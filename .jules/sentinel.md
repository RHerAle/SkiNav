## 2026-10-27 - Input Validation and Cost Bound Safeguards in Pathfinding Engine
**Vulnerability:** Unbounded departure time inputs and edge durations in the pathfinding and transit scheduling calculations (`calculateBusWait` and `calculateEdgeCost`) lacked bounds validation, allowing negative/overflow departure times or negative edge durations to corrupt A* graph costs or schedule calculations.
**Learning:** In routing and transit graph algorithms, calculations relying on mathematical modulo and ceiling operations (`ceil((departureTimeMinutes - start) / freq) * freq`) assume valid, non-negative inputs within real calendar horizons. Without explicit defensive bounds checking, unvalidated inputs could lead to negative wait times or heuristic contradictions.
**Prevention:** Always validate and clamp numeric domain inputs (departure times, frequencies, edge durations) at method boundaries and sanitize edge weights to strictly positive, finite numbers before priority queue traversal.
